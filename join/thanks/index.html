Your application is in. A person reads every one of these, which is why it takes a couple of days rather than a couple of seconds.

What happens from here

Within two business days

We check that you are an independent seller, that you run your own checkout, and that your products and prices are current. If something does not add up we ask you rather than guess.

Then we build your record

Your page, your catalog read from your own feed, and your listing in every category, type and brand index it belongs in.

You approve it before anyone sees it

Nothing about your store is published until you have read it. Billing starts the day you go live, not before.

Nothing arrived? Check your spam folder for our reply, and add our address to your contacts. If you hear nothing in three business days, chase us — something went wrong and we would rather know.

While you wait

Open your own store with JavaScript switched off. Whatever is still on screen is what a machine sees. It is the clearest two minutes you can spend on this.
