Showers4Less

Showers4Less is an independent online retailer in Denver, Colorado, operated by Aging Safely Baths. It specialises in accessible bathing: roll-in and barrier-free showers, ADA transfer showers, low-threshold walk-in systems, shower bases and pans, frameless and framed doors, steam generators and shower panels. The range also covers recovery equipment — infrared, outdoor and portable saunas, and cold plunge tubs. Products are sold DIY-ready or with installation, and the catalog runs from single fixtures to complete packages. Brands carried include ANZZI, Bestbath, SaunaSpa and SteamSpa.
