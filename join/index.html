What happens next

1. We check you are real

That you are an independent seller rather than a marketplace reseller front, that you run your own checkout, that the products and prices on your site are current, and that you ship somewhere reliably. Usually two business days.

2. We build your record

Server-rendered page, your facts written as text and marked up in schema.org, your catalog read from your own feed, a verification date, and your listing in every category, type and brand index it belongs in.

3. You approve it

You see the page before anyone else does. Nothing about your store gets published that you have not read. Anything wrong, we fix and re-date it.

4. Billing starts the day you are live

Not at application, not at approval. From then it is month to month, cancel in one click, and you keep your page export and your data on the way out.

What we never do

Yours, always

  • The customer, their email, and the reorder
  • The payment and the full margin
  • Your checkout, your returns, your service
  • Your data, exportable any time

Never ours to sell

  • A cut of your sales — there is none
  • Your position in the index
  • An unlabelled placement
  • Your details, to anyone

We will never ask you for your store password, your payment processor login, or your domain registrar credentials. We do not need them, and anyone claiming to be us who asks is not us.

Not sure it is worth $19.99 a month?

Open your own store in a browser with JavaScript switched off. Whatever is still on screen is roughly what a machine sees when it fetches your page. If that is a logo and a spinner, you already know why this exists.
