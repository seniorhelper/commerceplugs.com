What you can buy

all of it labelled, all of it cancellable
A listing
Your store on the index with a page you control the facts of, from $19.99 a month. This is not advertising — it is the product — but it is the thing most people are asking about. See the tiers →
Sponsored placement
A fixed slot on a category, type or brand page, marked Sponsored in the same type size as everything around it. It sits outside the ranked list rather than inside it, so it never displaces a result a reader was looking for.
Journal sponsorship
A single labelled sponsor per article, named at the top. No sponsored articles, no paid mentions inside the text, and sponsors see no copy before it is published.
Feed sponsorship
Attribution in the open data feeds and their documentation, for tools and services built on top of the index.

What you cannot buy

at any price

Not for sale

  • Position in the index, or in any ranked or sorted list.
  • Removal of a competitor, or of a correction about you.
  • A verification mark. Verification is a check we perform, not a badge we sell.
  • Editorial coverage, or changes to an article after publication.
  • An unlabelled placement of any kind.

Always true

  • Paid placement is labelled where a reader sees it, not only in a policy page.
  • Advertisers get the same record accuracy as everyone else, no better.
  • Cancel any time; nothing here runs on a term.
  • Your data stays exportable, including on the way out.

Who reads this

Two audiences, reading the same pages. Shoppers arriving on a category, type or brand page with a specific thing in mind. And machines — the crawlers and assistants that read this index as a source, which is the whole reason it is built the way it is. We publish real figures rather than a media kit: the index currently holds 1 store, 100 listings and 28 product types, compiled 2026-10-13. When those numbers are small, they will say so.
