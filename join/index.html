What we check

  • You are an independent store, not a marketplace reseller front
  • You control your own checkout
  • The products are real and the prices on your site are current
  • You ship to at least one country reliably

What happens next

We verify

Usually within two business days. If we cannot confirm something, we ask you rather than guess.

We write and publish your page

You read it and approve it before it goes live. Nothing about your store gets published that you have not seen.

Billing starts the day you are live

Not before. Month to month after that, cancel whenever.

We will never ask you for your store password, your payment processor login or your domain registrar credentials. If anyone claiming to be us does, it is not us.
